# AI Usage and Declaration

## FIRE FUSION
### GLOBAL SYNTHETIC SQUAD (G.S.S.)

This document transparently describes how Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools were used during the development, visualization, documentation, and presentation of the FIRE FUSION project.

## 1. Our Commitment to Transparency

GLOBAL SYNTHETIC SQUAD (G.S.S.) believes in responsible, ethical, and transparent use of Artificial Intelligence.

FIRE FUSION is a team-driven project. The core project idea, conceptualization, system vision, workflow design, and overall project direction were developed by our team.

AI tools were used as supporting technologies to help transform our original ideas into understandable explanations, visual demonstrations, presentations, documentation, and implementation assistance.

AI was not the source of our original project concept.

## 2. AI Tools Used

The following AI-powered tools were used during different stages of the project:

| AI Tool | Purpose of Use |
|----------|---------------|
| ChatGPT | Project explanation, research assistance, presentation content, video prompts, narration scripts, documentation, README preparation, code assistance, debugging support, and image generation assistance. |
| Google Flow | AI-assisted video generation and scene visualization for project presentations and demonstrations. |
| Google Gemini | Research assistance, project planning, writing, and content development. |
| Google AI Studio | AI-assisted experimentation and development support. |
| Canva | Presentation design, graphics, visual assets, and AI-assisted image generation. |
| ElevenLabs | AI-assisted voice generation and narration for project videos. |
| CapCut | Video editing, clip assembly, narration integration, and final video production. |

Additional AI-assisted creative and development tools may have been used where appropriate.

## 3. AI-Assisted Development and Content Creation

AI tools assisted our team in several areas:

- Explaining technical concepts in accessible language.
- Organizing project presentation content.
- Preparing presentation slides and supporting visual materials.
- Generating cinematic video prompts and scene descriptions.
- Creating AI-assisted visual demonstrations of satellite and ground monitoring concepts.
- Preparing voiceover scripts and narration.
- Improving project documentation and GitHub README content.
- Assisting with coding, debugging, and technical problem-solving.
- Supporting research and understanding of relevant technologies.

AI-generated visuals, narration, and video scenes are used as presentation and explanatory materials. They should not be interpreted as direct evidence of actual satellite operations, real-world wildfire detection, or measured prototype performance.

## 4. Original Human Contribution

The core intellectual and conceptual work behind FIRE FUSION belongs to GLOBAL SYNTHETIC SQUAD (G.S.S.).

Our team independently developed the project's original idea and overall vision, including:

- The original FIRE FUSION concept and problem-solving approach.
- The idea of combining satellite-based observations with ground-based monitoring.
- The conceptual workflow connecting the land demonstration prototype with satellite observation and data processing.
- The project architecture and system-level design decisions.
- The conceptual approach to satellite data filtering and normalization.
- The overall monitoring, analysis, and data-fusion workflow.
- The land demonstration concept and its role in explaining the monitoring system.
- Team decisions, project direction, experimentation, prototype work, and testing activities.

AI assistance was used to help communicate, visualize, document, and support the development of these team-created ideas.

## 5. Human Oversight and Verification

The team reviewed AI-assisted outputs and verified information before incorporating it into project materials.

We recognize that AI systems can generate inaccurate information, incorrect code, misleading visualizations, or unsupported technical claims.

Therefore:

- AI-generated information was reviewed by team members.
- Technical decisions remained under human responsibility.
- Project concepts and implementation decisions were determined by the team.
- AI-generated visuals were treated as illustrative representations.
- Claims about project functionality and performance were not intended to be established solely through AI-generated content.

## 6. Responsible AI Usage

We are committed to:

- Transparent disclosure of AI assistance.
- Maintaining human ownership of project ideas and decisions.
- Avoiding misleading representations of AI-generated demonstrations.
- Reviewing AI-assisted technical information.
- Respecting applicable licenses, platform policies, and intellectual property rights.
- Using AI as a supporting tool rather than a substitute for independent thinking, creativity, and engineering judgment.

## 7. Project-Specific AI Disclosure

FIRE FUSION combines a team-developed environmental monitoring concept with AI-assisted communication and visualization.

AI tools helped us bring our ideas to life through presentations, visual storytelling, generated video scenes, narration, documentation, and development support.

The original project concept, system vision, land-to-satellite monitoring approach, and conceptual data-processing workflow were developed by GLOBAL SYNTHETIC SQUAD (G.S.S.).

Any AI-generated satellite imagery, forest scenes, prototype illustrations, dashboards, or animations presented in project videos are illustrative unless explicitly identified as actual project data or implementation outputs.
